# Product Positioning

Updated: 2026-04-28

## Short Definition

TMS v2 is an operational transport management platform for transport companies and shippers, with a compliance-first roadmap for electronic transport documents.

It is not just a route planner and not just an EDO cabinet. The product combines operational transport flow, fleet control, waybills, inspections, repairs, finance, and transport-document readiness.

## Current Position

```text
Operational TMS with a compliance-first transport platform trajectory
```

## Strong Existing Areas

- Orders, trips, assignment, waybills, inspections, delivery, and billing.
- Fleet, drivers, trailers, readiness, repairs, maintenance, fuel, and downtime foundation.
- Finance, tariffs, invoices, acts, KPI, and export foundation.
- Transport dossier and persisted internal transport documents.
- ETRN XML/export foundation and provider-style exchange attempts, receipts, and callbacks.
- Multi-organization scope and access guards.
- Production-oriented deployment with backup, rollback, health, and readiness checks.

## Not Yet Claimed

- Legally complete EDO/EPD exchange through an accredited operator.
- Production GIS EPD contour.
- KEP/UKEP, MChD, certificate lifecycle, and signer authority verification.
- Full route optimization comparable to ANTOR or Yandex Routing.
- Mobile driver app inside this v2 workspace.
- Enterprise-grade observability and SLA operations.

## Target Tracks

### Track A: Compliance-First TMS

Primary target: companies preparing for mandatory electronic transport documents.

Core investment:

- EPD/ETRN operator integration.
- Electronic waybill and transport document signing.
- Driver mobile document flow.
- QR/offline document access.
- Provider receipts, statuses, and archive.

### Track B: Operational Fleet TMS

Primary target: companies with own fleet or mixed fleet operations.

Core investment:

- Mobile driver execution.
- GPS/Wialon/GLONASS monitoring.
- Route plan/fact.
- Fuel norms and fleet health.
- Repair stock and procurement.
